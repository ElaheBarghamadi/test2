feat(grading): the teacher's pen outranks the key, and a result shows only what it is told to

Marking
- `grade_answer` reads a manual score first, on every question type: a keyed answer can now be corrected
  in place instead of editing the question and silently moving the whole class's marks with it.
- A question the student left blank takes a mark too - the grade endpoints create the answer row instead
  of refusing, and `manual_score: null` hands the question back to the key.
- A note is its own gesture: a `feedback`-only write leaves the number alone, and the mark the key gave
  stays visible beside the teacher's ("کلید ۲") so an override reads as a decision, not a lost grade.
- Both marking screens carry an editor on every row; the zero-fill wand refuses to touch a keyed
  question, so a keyed screen cannot silently zero a class. Correct/incorrect tallies follow the key
  while the marks follow the pen, which is what the two numbers mean.

Publication
- `ExamSettings.result_detail` is a four-rung ladder: score only, their own sheet, sheet plus the
  teacher's notes, sheet plus notes plus the key. Null keeps the old meaning of `show_correct_answers`,
  so an exam that never chose behaves exactly as before.
- While any answer is still queued the payload is cut at the smallest rung, so a half-marked sheet
  cannot read as a wrong grade; `detail_level` says what the student got and why.
- The rung is chosen in the publish dialog and in the builder's settings step off one shared ladder, and
  duplication carries it over.

Exam chrome
- one bar with four rounded corners at the top of the phone layout, and nothing docked at the bottom.
